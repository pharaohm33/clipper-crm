# Meta ads optimization: email set

Not wired into the CRM yet. This is the wording, adapted from "Strategy 1", with the offer changed to Meta ads optimization.

Fill-ins (the CRM would supply these):
- `{first}` the person's first name
- `{company}` their company
- `{product}` your product name (assumed "AI Ads Optimization" from your sign up page address: change it if it's different)
- `{ads_count}` how many active ads you saw in their Meta Ad Library
- `{link}` your sign up page: https://portal.aiadsoptimization.com/signup
- `{sender}` your name

Every cold email also carries your postal address and an opt-out line (added automatically by the sender).

Two lines in the original were left out of the cold version on purpose. "Based on what we discussed" and "My team had sent me over your info saying you were interested" tell a stranger that you already spoke or that they asked to hear from you. If that isn't true, it reads as a false claim, and it is the fastest way to get reported as spam. Those wordings are kept below only for people you really did speak with.

Also check that you can back up the numbers you state. "10 to 20 percent savings", "we're extremely confident", and "proof every day" are claims. Say them only if your product delivers them, or soften them to "many advertisers see" / "I'd estimate".

## 1. Cold first email (no prior contact)

Subject: Cutting wasted Meta ad spend at {company}

Hey {first},

I looked at {company}'s Meta Ad Library and saw about {ads_count} active ads, so I wanted to reach out.

{product} connects to your existing Meta ads account, monitors your campaigns 24/7, and automatically cuts wasteful ad spend.

There is no setup fee, and we only charge 20% of the money we actually save you. We send proof of the savings every day. If we don't save you anything, you don't pay anything.

Want me to send over the details? Just reply here.

- {sender}
